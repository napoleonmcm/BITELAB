Photo tips: daylight near a window, plain dark surface or wooden board,
shoot combos from above at 45 degrees, wipe plate edges, and compress
each file to under 300 KB at squoosh.app before uploading.

THIS DESIGN NEEDS CUT-OUT PHOTOS
The food "pops out" of the cards, so each photo must be a PNG with a
transparent background (no plate edge, no table):
  1. Take the photo in good daylight, shooting from a 45 degree angle.
  2. Remove the background free with remove.bg, Canva's Background Remover,
     or on iPhone: press and hold the food in Photos > Copy/Share subject.
  3. Save as .png with the exact file name above, then crop close to the food.

New items you add from the dashboard: put the photo here and type its file
name in the item's "Photo file name or link" box.
